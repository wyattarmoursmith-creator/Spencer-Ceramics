Before you write

The usual questions.

Where do you ship from, and how long does it take?

From Auckland, with tracking once it ships: NZ Couriers within New Zealand, Australia Post within Australia, DHL or FedEx everywhere else. Delivery times depend on the carrier and, for international orders, on customs.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, worldwide. Import duties, customs charges and taxes in your country are yours to cover.

My piece arrived broken. What now?

Keep all the packaging, photograph the box and the piece, and email shop@craigspencerceramics.com within 48 hours. We'll repair, replace or refund.

Can I return or cancel?

All sales are final and orders can't be changed once placed. Damaged, defective or incorrect pieces are always put right, and your Consumer Guarantees Act rights are unaffected.

Will my piece look exactly like the photo?

Close, but not identical. Every piece is handmade, and small variations in colour, glaze, texture and size are part of the work, not defects.

Do you take commissions or collaborations?

Yes. Bespoke pieces and brand collaborations are agreed separately, with their own scope, timeline and deposit. Use the form here, or email projects@craigspencerceramics.com with your project details.

Do you offer wholesale?

No. We sell directly to customers and don't currently offer wholesale pricing. Stockists are listed on the Studio page.

Are the pieces food-safe?

Yes. Every glaze is food-safe, unglazed surfaces are sealed with a non-toxic, water-resistant finish, and all pieces are fired to 1220°C. More on materials.
