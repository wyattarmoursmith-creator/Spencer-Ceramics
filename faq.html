Shipping & FAQ

Good to know.

Shipping

Carriers & tracking

Most orders are shipped via NZ Couriers or, within Australia, Australia Post. For international shipping we use either DHL or FedEx International, and deliveries typically do not require a signature. Tracking information will be provided once your order has shipped.

For international orders, the customer is responsible for any applicable import taxes, customs duties, or other fees charged by the destination country.

Packaging

Because ceramics are fragile, every item is securely wrapped and packed using protective materials. We make every reasonable effort to ensure your order arrives safely.

Orders may arrive in separate packages if they contain items stored or shipped separately.

Damaged or broken items

All sales are final, including custom and sample pieces. Once an order enters production, it cannot be modified or canceled. We do our best to package and pack as best we can, and we will review each broken delivery individually and with good intent.

If your item arrives damaged or contains defective parts, please retain all original packaging and document the issue immediately upon delivery. Damage or defects must be reported within 48 hours, along with photos of both the packaging and the product.

If a repair or replacement is not possible, a refund will be issued.

Please email shop@craigspencerceramics.com to report an issue.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses

Please check your delivery address carefully before completing your order.

If an order is returned because of an incorrect or incomplete address, the customer will be responsible for reshipping. If the order cannot be reshipped, any refund may be reduced by the original shipping costs and return fees.

Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to update your address. We cannot guarantee that changes can be made after an order has been dispatched.

International orders & customs

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, brokerage fees, or other charges imposed by the destination country.

We are not responsible for delays caused by customs. If an international order is refused or abandoned because of unpaid customs charges, any refund may be reduced by shipping, return, and other costs incurred.

Return shipping costs

Unless an item is defective, incorrect, or damaged on arrival, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs. We recommend using a tracked and insured shipping service. We cannot be responsible for items lost or damaged while being returned.

Handmade variations

Our ceramics are handmade, so minor variations in colour, glaze, texture, shape, size, and finish are normal and are not considered defects. Small marks, pinholes, speckles, kiln variations, and slight irregularities are part of the character of handmade work. These variations will not normally qualify an item for a return or refund.

Wholesale

At Craig Spencer Ceramics we specialise in direct-to-customer sales and don't currently offer wholesale pricing.

Collaborations

We collaborate with brands that share our values, vision, and commitment to thoughtful design. Each collaboration is carefully selected based on how well the brand aligns with our creative direction and purpose. Through these partnerships, we create meaningful, unique ceramic pieces that reflect the spirit of both brands.

Please email us at projects@craigspencerceramics.com and share your brand vision and project details with us.
