On making Auckland · MMXXVI

Objects of beauty
and simplicity.

Craig throws, trims and glazes each piece himself, start to finish. What the kiln adds, he leaves in.

For Craig, ceramics and design share the same concerns: material, colour, texture. Useful things, quietly made, to live with for years.

Good to know

Before you buy.

Where do you ship from, and how?

From the studio in Auckland. Within New Zealand we use NZ Couriers, within Australia, Australia Post, and everywhere else DHL or FedEx. You'll get tracking as soon as your order ships.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, worldwide. Any import duties, customs charges or taxes in your country are yours to cover, and we can't control customs delays. If a parcel is refused because charges weren't paid, the refund is reduced by the shipping and return costs.

What if my piece arrives broken?

Keep all the packaging, photograph both the box and the piece, and email shop@craigspencerceramics.com within 48 hours of delivery. We look at every case individually. If a repair or replacement isn't possible, you'll be refunded.

Can I return or cancel an order?

All sales are final, including custom and sample pieces, and an order can't be changed once it's placed. Damaged, defective or incorrect pieces are always put right, and nothing here affects your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act.

Why doesn't my piece look exactly like the photo?

Because it's handmade. Small differences in colour, glaze, texture, shape and size are normal, as are pinholes, speckles and kiln marks. They're the character of the work, not defects, and they don't qualify a piece for a return.

Are the pieces food-safe?

Yes. Every glaze is food-safe, and unglazed surfaces are sealed with Liquid Quartz®, a non-toxic, water-resistant finish that's safe for everyday food contact once cured. All pieces are fired to 1220°C. More on materials.
